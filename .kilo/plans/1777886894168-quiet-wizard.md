## 4. Architecture Improvement Opportunities

**High-impact, low-cost**:
1. **Stochastic depth**: Add `drop_path_rate` (default 0.0) to ParagonSR3 init; apply in GatedRepConv (between dw and SCA) and possibly attention. Improves generalization, negligible TRT impact.
2. **Video recurrence training fix**: Recommend separate `VideoMultiScaleSRModel` that maintains `prev_feat` state across mini-batches during training. Or use a video dataset loader that returns consecutive frames. Without this, video variant's recurrence is dead weight.
3. **Token count reduction for video**: Reduce `num_tokens` from 64 → 32 in video variant TokenDictionaryCA (fewer global tokens). Slight quality trade-off for speed; can be configurable.
4. **Window attention heads**: Allow reducing heads (4 → 2) in video variant for extra speed.

**Medium-impact**:
5. **Loss tuning**: Test Charbonnier vs L1+MSSIM, weight decay sweep.
6. **Detail gain**: The `detail_gain` parameter (0.1/0.2) is learnable. Check if it converges near init or drifts; consider making it fixed.

**Already optimized**:
- RepConv fusion is excellent.
- Sparse beacons are well-placed.
- Hierarchical fusion is included.
